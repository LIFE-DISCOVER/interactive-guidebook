{% translate "What is the DISCOVER Guidebook?" %}
{% translate "Supporting the energy transition through community action" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} The DISCOVER project (funded under EU Horizon) helps local energy agencies across Europe support citizens who want to start or join a Community Energy Project (CEP). These projects — from solar cooperatives to renewable energy communities — play a key role in the clean energy transition. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Initiating a CEP is complex. Founders face technical, legal, financial, and social challenges that require expertise across many disciplines. Energy agency managers (the intermediaries) are uniquely positioned to guide initiators through this journey, but they need structured tools to do so effectively. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} This interactive guidebook translates the DISCOVER methodology into a practical day-to-day tool: a searchable service catalog, project tracking, and phase-based guidance — all in one place. {% endblocktranslate %}
